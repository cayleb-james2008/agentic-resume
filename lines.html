Buy on contract

Purchasing contracts let you buy at pre-determined discounts, and the bigger the order, the bigger the discount. If you qualify for the Connecticut State Contract or buy through MHEC, you get that pricing without going through the bid process.

A bid has costs people forget: preparing the bid package, answering questions, reviewing submissions and comparing products offered as “equals”. And the winning bid might not be the product you wanted, or the dealer you wanted to work with. Buying on contract avoids all of that.

As of January 1, 2026, the Connecticut Department of Administrative Services (DAS) adopted the Massachusetts Higher Education Consortium (MHEC) cooperative purchasing agreement for office furniture and architectural products. Eligible Connecticut agencies and organizations can buy from contract-approved manufacturers at competitively negotiated prices. The contract runs through December 31, 2030.
