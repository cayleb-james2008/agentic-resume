Summary

AI agent systems engineer and technical founder. I have spent the past year on agentic systems and harness engineering, starting while I was finishing high school, where I was also wrestling team captain for two years. For most of that stretch I worked 13 to 14 hours a day on it, around school, my job and wrestling. I work across several harnesses instead of just one: OpenCode and DeepSeek Harness, Claude Desktop and Duet, Gemini Antigravity, Freebuff, ChatGPT Codex and Claude Code. I pick whichever has usage left and fits the job. Right now most of the work runs through Muse, Claude Duet and the DSH Fleet I am finishing. Three systems make up most of it. Ergon's employee agents set themselves up: the agent watches how someone works, asks them targeted questions, learns the role and keeps a memory of the person, adding skills and tools as it goes. The DSH Fleet is a distributed orchestration layer: it runs dynamic swarms across desktop, virtual machine and browser surfaces, lets outside agents join through Slack, and improves its own workflows, skills and plugin behaviour from the results of finished jobs. OpenBell is my trading research system: I set the weights of tiny models directly, freeze their predictions before scoring, and score them in time order with costs counted. 1,200+ GitHub contributions in the last year. 4,000+ automated tests across five shipped systems.
