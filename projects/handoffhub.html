at a glance

What it reviews

HandoffHub fetches current public records from one federal agency, then reads the matching document from the official government text service. The recorded read used document 2026-19222, published 2026-09-18, and both requests returned HTTP 200.

Paragraphs that need personal-data screening are left out. The reuse notice is stored with the source record, and no redirect or challenge page is followed.

Where a person takes over

A public-policy reviewer checks the official edition and decides whether the quoted text answers the question. No real internal owner is attached to this handoff.

It is not internal knowledge, an employer-approved policy, proof of workplace permissions, or evidence of a real owner. The full staff job stays unverified until an authorized workplace owner and permission-scoped records exist.

what is not verified

  • Public document text only; no internal knowledge or workplace permissions were reviewed.
  • No real internal owner is attached, so the staff handoff stays unverified.
  • No message is sent and no account changes.
  • The reader should consult the official edition for legal research.
