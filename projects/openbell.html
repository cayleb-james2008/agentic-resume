at a glance

Two desks, one app

OpenBell is one app with two trading desks and a game-style dashboard on top of both. Most trading screens are rows of numbers; OpenBell is built the other way. The dashboard is the product: each desk, its positions and its state read at a glance, the way a game display does.

The futures desk works MES setups as cards on the dashboard rather than as orders typed into a broker screen. It runs on a FundedNext evaluation account in simulation, with a daily loss stop, a daily profit stop, a per-day trade cap and a hard flat-by time. The meme desk is the Solana side. It runs several strategy slots in parallel, each holding an equal share of the desk's capital and counting that share as its own 100% for sizing. A strategy gets up to three iterations to prove itself; a fourth failure wipes the slot and replaces it with a new strategy.

Rules the desks run under

Every strategy change goes through build, check and arm. Nothing is hot-patched into a live desk. The checker is never the maker: a strategy is graded by a fresh pass against fixed criteria, not by the session that wrote it. Risk rails are set before a session starts and are not widened mid-session.

The two desks keep their own engines and never import from each other. They meet in exactly one file: the meme desk writes a small state snapshot every fifteen seconds, and the dashboard reads that file and nothing else from the desk. A missing, stale or malformed file shows as a state on the meme tab; it never blanks or breaks the futures side.

what is not verified

  • The futures desk trades a FundedNext evaluation account in simulation; this page makes no performance claim and shows no result.
  • The repository says the meme desk trades live on Solana at small size; its results live in the repository's reports, not here.
  • Risk rails and the three-iteration rule are the desk's documented rules, not a claim that every session follows them perfectly.
  • The code repository is private; this page is the public record.
  • No claim is made about profitability, capital growth or live trading outcomes.
