at a glance

What Ergon is

Ergon is the formula for company-wide personalised AI agents: a template and a set of creation blueprints. It ships no pre-packaged apps. Each company gets its own separate private GitHub repository holding its full personalised suite — its apps, skills, tools and each of its personalised agents — as a cloud home that stays separate from this platform repository and from every other company.

It is one merged project. It follows the DeepSeek Harness pattern at company scale: an agent that customises itself through conversation, governed by a staging-to-promotion check. The legacy 1.5.5 source tree is still in the repository as history. Its old base, OpenClaw, is superseded, and Outis, the former management layer, was retired on 1 November 2026.

Where it stands

RHL is the proposed first deployment of Ergon, and nothing more. The concept was discussed there and drew interest, but RHL has not authorised or implemented an AI integration, and no Ergon infrastructure is in production there. The one RHL-specific build is a standalone warehouse board, built as a non-AI operational showcase at Roy's request. It is a demonstration artifact, not an Ergon company app, not an official RHL system, and not evidence of adoption.

The simulation programme has run since 4 November 2026. Three simulated companies — Meridian Van Line, Bayside Dental and Northgate Law — run full operations with detailed employee personas against the baseline. Every failure becomes a numbered BASELINE defect, fixed by one side and checked by another. Run 1 passed after the instance-shape fix, and a 36-scenario pack now exercises the subagent library, the skill library, custom apps and database maintenance.

Access is controlled at the hosting layer behind a zero-trust gate: employees sign in through the company's identity provider, and the origin is reachable only through the gate. There are no Ergon-issued employee keys and no Ergon passwords. The gate is a design decision with a written plan; standing it up is still staged behind the owner's own acts.

what is not verified

  • Windows installation, enrollment, reboot reconnection and live connector account use still need live checks; source tests and compilation do not establish them.
  • No Ergon deployment exists anywhere yet; every company suite is created when that company is set up.
  • RHL has not authorised or implemented an AI integration, and the warehouse board is a non-AI demo.
  • The access gate has a written plan but has not been stood up.
  • Ergon is not declared finished; the baseline is still being hardened through the simulation programme.
  • Prices are unset and none are published. The old 1.4.9 installer record is history, not the current state.
