Independent engineering project · October 13, 2026

Industry AI Suite

10 tools that review documents and data. Every finding shows its source, and a person makes the final call.

What it does

Each tool handles one kind of review: finance, research, support, operations or security. It reports what it found, where it came from, how sure it is, and what a person should do next. Company files stay on the local computer.

Review to receipt

Save a short record of each review, mark it checked or send it back, then export or delete it. Raw imported files are never kept.

Local model drafting

An optional AI on the same computer can write a draft from the findings. If it can't cite its sources, or isn't running, the draft stops: nothing is made up and nothing goes to the cloud. The review still finishes without it.

Try it

Setup steps to run it on your own computer. The lab replays ten recorded runs.

Verification and limits

October 13: 382 automated tests, 178 sub-tests and 19 browser tests passed. The local AI wasn't running for this test. Source authority and full organization outcomes remain UNVERIFIED in each record. The 2026-09-26 AI test record stays as dated history.
