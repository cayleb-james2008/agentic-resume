Tessa + PipThe repair needs a part.

Tessa Rowan coordinates repairs at the fictional Brackenvale Workshop. A customer needs a pickup date for a desk lamp. The SW-17 part has not arrived.

Tessa + PipA forecast is not receipt.

The supplier note expects Thursday 15 October, 10:00. Pip keeps that forecast separate from confirmed part receipt.

Tessa + PipA conditional hold.

Hold Thursday provisionally. Confirm only after the part arrives and the repair is tested. Tessa reviews the plan. Nothing is sent.

Mina + FolioThe approval is for another proof.

Mina Solis prepares releases at fictional Larkspur Field Guides. The newest proof is v5 with 52 pages; the older approval covers text in v4 with 48 pages. Folio cannot silently approve the new layout.

Mina + FolioMatch version and scope.

A staged update from editor Anika Frost approves the full v5 proof. Folio matches the 52-page version to its exact approval, rather than treating latest as approved.

Mina + FolioPrepare, then authorize.

Folio assembles a manifest containing the v5 proof record, print specs and approval. Mina prepares the pack. Production lead Rowan Pike authorizes any external release. This is a source-record illustration, not an actual downloadable proof or printer send.

Dev + LoamThe counts disagree.

Dev Ellis coordinates stock at fictional Copper Finch Supply. The ledger says 36; an unsigned shelf note says 32. A signed recount is required before new allocation.

Dev + LoamProtect earlier reservations.

A staged signed count from Sam Alder confirms 32. Subtract 10 locked reservations, leaving 22 available. Unreceived inbound stock is excluded.

Dev + LoamAllocate without unauthorized partials.

Order 204 needs 14 and is fully allocated in the draft. Order 205 needs 9, so the remaining 8 are earmarked pending review, not issued as a partial pick. No units remain free, and later orders are not fulfilled by skipping this constraint. Dev reviews the plan. Nothing ships.
