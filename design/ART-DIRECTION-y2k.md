# Art direction — Catppuccin Mocha + nomad mark (2026-09-22)

## Vibe (current lock)
**Minimalistic professional** on Catppuccin Mocha dark purple. Mauve/lavender line work, generous negative space, calm surfaces.
Heavy Y2K sticker-grunge is **not** the primary brand treatment (grit assets may remain in `assets/img/y2k/` for optional texture, not chrome).

## Palette
Bases `#11111b` / `#1e1e2e` with mauve `#cba6f7`, lavender `#b4befe`, pink `#f5c2e7`. Prefer thin borders and quiet panels over neon glow stacks.

## Character / logo
**Cyber-nomad as clean outline mark** — hood, pack, handheld terminal strokes.
Primary asset: `assets/img/brand/nomad-outline-logo.png` (clean mauve outline). Vector companion: `assets/img/logo/nomad-mark.svg` + favicon.
Do **not** use coffee-cup imagery. Do **not** use the painted sticker PNG as the dock/hero brand mark.

## Motion
anime.js for light entrances; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. No RGB-glitch mascot loops on the logo.

## Video / hero
Real `<video>` + minimal poster. CRT frame is a quiet bezel, not a grit collage.
