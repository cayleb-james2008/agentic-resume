BacktestGuard

Checks the year order of a split of 15 public World Bank GDP observations and warns about future-year leakage.

at a glance

What it reviews

BacktestGuard looks for research mistakes such as using later information in an earlier decision. Its live command reads 15 actual World Bank U.S. GDP observations and keeps the existing default split: training 2011–2020, holdout 2021–2025.

A person can pass a different cutoff year. The chosen year must be present in the live observations and must leave both sides of the split non-empty; otherwise the app reports the source as unavailable and does not fall back to another split.

Where a person takes over

The output names the cutoff, both year lists, the source details, and a warning about a future year. A quant research reviewer reads those and decides what the check means for the experiment.

The check is not a completed backtest. The external experiment record, the target or return definition, and the source release timing are not inspected, so the full research review stays unverified until a person supplies them.

what is not verified

  • Public GDP observations only; no experiment record was read.
  • No backtest result is claimed, and the check is not a completed backtest.
  • The full research review stays unverified until a person supplies the experiment record, the return definition, and the source release timing.
  • There is no broker, order, strategy execution, or publication step.
