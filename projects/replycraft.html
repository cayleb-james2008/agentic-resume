at a glance

What it reviews

ReplyCraft is built to draft a cited reply to one question. Its normal command runs a clearly labelled public-policy sample on the first eligible OPM rule or proposed rule in the live list (the public proposal numbered 2026-19222 in the recorded read) and cites the safe date section of the official text for a person to review.

The sample is a question about a public proposal, not a customer case. Text that would need privacy screening is left out, and the draft never says it is the organization's own support policy.

Where a person takes over

A policy reviewer checks the document and decides whether the sample helps. Any real use needs an actual consent-authorized case and an approved internal support policy; even then, the consent and policy-owner claims need independent confirmation.

No message is sent and no account is changed. A local import is limited to a bundle file of no more than 64 KB.

what is not verified

  • The sample question is not a customer case and no customer consent was supplied.
  • No approved internal support policy exists for the sample.
  • No message is sent, no account changes, and there is no send capability.
  • Even with a local import, the consent and policy-owner claims need independent confirmation.
