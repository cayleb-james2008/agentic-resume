Peter joined the company in 1989, became Vice President in 2000 and took over as President & CEO in 2023. He works to build and keep strong relationships with our business partners as the company grows. A cancer survivor, Peter gives back to the Dana-Farber Cancer Institute. He is a graduate of St. Anselm College.
