at a glance

What the suite is

Industry AI Suite is a working Python workbench for ten research and review tasks. Shared parts collect source information, attach citations, keep a minimized review history, and support export or deletion. Organization bundles run locally on safe examples or permitted de-identified exports.

The ten tools cover finance reconciliation, macro research, research-integrity checks, public-policy drafting, workplace knowledge handoffs, security triage, content review, public-repository research, employee onboarding review and watch-only wallet review. Each page on this site describes one of them.

Evidence, drafts and the human decision

Each tool reports what it found, where it came from, how sure it is, and what a person should do next. A saved receipt can be marked checked or sent back, then exported or deleted. Raw imported files are not kept. Company files stay on the local computer.

An optional AI on the same computer can write a draft from the findings. If it cannot cite its sources, or is not running, the draft stops: nothing is invented and nothing goes to the cloud. The review still finishes without it. The suite never sends, publishes, posts, trades or changes an account.

Verified implementation and limits

The merged implementation passed 382 tests, 178 subtests and 19 browser checks on 13 October 2026. A dated model record from 26 September 2026 (2026-09-26) stays as history in the repository's evidence/ai-witness-20260926 folder. Passing tests and a successful source response do not prove a complete business workflow or live AI.

Full organization workflows remain UNVERIFIED until private records, source authority and a human approver are available. The hosted lab replays dated public results; it does not fetch fresh sources. No company deployment, customer use or commercial outcome is claimed.
