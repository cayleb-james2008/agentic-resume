### Accessibility, responsive behavior, and performance

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA; this is a requirement, **not a conformance claim**. Check 1.1.1 (meaningful alt text), 1.3.1 (structure), 1.4.3 (contrast), 1.4.4 (text resize), 1.4.10 (reflow), 2.1.1 (keyboard), 2.4.6 (headings/labels), 2.4.7 (focus visible), 2.4.11 (focus not obscured), 2.5.8 (target size), and 4.1.2 (control names/roles/states). Use text labels, not color alone, for `SOURCE VERIFIED`, `WIP · INCOMPLETE`, `UNVERIFIED`, and `AI UNVERIFIED`.
- All navigation, links, and controls work by keyboard; no hover-only information or traps. Keep a visible focus ring. Check contrast (4.5:1 normal text; 3:1 large text), 200% zoom and reflow, and use at least 44×44 CSS px touch targets as the project’s stricter target.
- At **390 px**, use one column, stable gutters, wrapped code/IDs, stacked evidence fields, usable navigation, and no horizontal overflow or hidden content. At desktop **1440×900**, preserve an airy editorial measure and clear evidence hierarchy. Reserve image dimensions; lazy-load below-fold media.
- No heavy video on first load unless a measured case justifies it. No remote fonts, scripts, styles, or runtime. Lab goal: LCP ≤2.5s and CLS ≤0.1; report INP ≤200ms only with suitable field evidence. CAP-14 still requires three mobile baseline/final runs per home and ten project surfaces, recording tool/version, LCP, CLS, and responsiveness proxy; final medians must be no worse than comparable versioned v5 baseline.
