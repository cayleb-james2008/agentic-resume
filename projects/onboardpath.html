at a glance

What it reviews

OnboardPath is built to help a person read public federal rule text that may bear on an onboarding review. The recorded read used document 2026-19222, published 2026-09-18, and both the list request and the official text returned HTTP 200.

By default it reviews the first eligible Rule or Proposed Rule; a named document must already appear in the fresh response. The review keeps short safe excerpts, fixed section citations, dates, and the reuse notice.

Where a person takes over

A person checks the official edition and decides whether the text is relevant. Employee-specific service, the employer's own policy, and permission-scoped records still have to come from the employer.

The text is a public document excerpt, not HR policy, legal advice, employee evidence, hiring guidance, permission proof, or an action. The full onboarding review stays unverified until authorized records and an approved employer policy exist.

what is not verified

  • Public proposed-rule text only; no employer policy or employee records.
  • No employee-specific answer; the review is not HR policy, legal advice, or hiring guidance.
  • The full onboarding review stays unverified until authorized records, an approved policy, and HR review exist.
  • No action capability: the app cannot change a record or send a message.
