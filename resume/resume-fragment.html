OpenBell case study

Weight-space programme: instead of training black-box models, I hand-author tiny model weights directly (linear specialists), freeze the prediction in writing before scoring, and judge on strict rules — no money test until the training win rate clears the breakeven for its exit shape. Live desks run small real capital with reconciled accounting and pre-registered kill conditions.
