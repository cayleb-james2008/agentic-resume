### DSH Fleet - Distributed, Self-Improving Agent Infrastructure

Python, TypeScript

[DSH Fleet case study](https://cayleb-james2008.github.io/agentic-resume/projects/dsh-fleet.html)

- Control plane spanning desktop, Windows VM, phone VM and browser surfaces. Slack is the open coordination layer: my agents and bring-your-own external agents share one ticket, context and outcome protocol. A lead forms a task-shaped swarm per objective — no standing bench — dissolving after one auditable outcome.

- Each objective gets a dynamic swarm shaped to the task, formed around a lead: preferences, current skills, research, model routing. Every session is defined, material actions need approval, and the swarm dissolves after one recorded outcome.

- Job evidence feeds Omni Loop revisions to workflows, plugin behaviour, the skill library and the loop itself. Promotion needs a fresh check measuring a win on a fixed scorecard (checker-pass rate, cost per checker-passed task) plus a frozen regression set; failures archived, changes reversible. Inside every swarm: preferences first, current skill second, raw model knowledge last. The fleet has caught its own swarms filing false 'done' reports — each found by re-checking the primary source.

### OpenBell - Weight-Space Training & Evaluation for Trading Models

Python, Rust

[OpenBell case study](https://cayleb-james2008.github.io/agentic-resume/projects/openbell.html)

- Direct weight-space construction: low-dimensional weights for tiny specialist models, not prompted strategies. Weights and predictions frozen before scoring: inspectable, falsifiable.

- Adversarial evaluation: chronological splits, transaction costs, negative results retained, pre-registered kill rules, no money test until training clears the exit-geometry breakeven. Distillation failed on both benchmark instruments; directly authored weights produced the strongest results — one Nasdaq configuration net-positive on historical data.

- Kalshi, futures and meme desks: live, capital-isolated proving grounds — parallel slots, 2x ladder runs, reconciled accounting, small real capital, pre-registered kill conditions.
