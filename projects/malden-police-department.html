Malden Police Department

Malden, MA

We worked with Flansburgh Architects and the Malden Police Department to select and specify the furniture for the department's new building. A public safety building needs furniture that is comfortable but durable.

There is a lobby and reception area, public meeting rooms, private and open offices, and specialty rooms such as interrogation and roll call rooms.

These are our renderings and design boards for the project, not photos of the finished rooms.

Scope
24,000 sq ft new construction.
