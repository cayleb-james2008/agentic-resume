Live trading house · November 6, 2026

OpenBell

All of my trading and finance work under one house: a Kalshi prediction-market desk, a futures desk and a meme-coin desk.

Three desks, one architecture

Every desk runs on the same architecture: parallel strategy slots splitting the equity evenly. Each strategy runs in its own slot with its own budget and scoreboard, so a failing strategy can never quietly drain the others.

The slot method

Each slot iterates on measured results. A strategy that fails its pre-set kill conditions is stopped, wiped and replaced with a brand-new approach — the slot has to earn its place again.

The weight-space programme

Instead of training black-box models, I hand-author tiny model weights directly (linear specialists), freeze the prediction in writing before scoring, and judge on strict rules — no money test until the training win rate clears the breakeven for its exit shape. Conventional distillation failed on both test instruments; the hand-authored weights passed, and the best configuration was net-positive on historical data.

Books and rails

Live desks run small real capital with pre-registered kill conditions. I caught and fixed an inflated scoreboard that counted payouts without costs; the books now reconcile against full venue history.

Evidence

Live desks, small real capital. Reconciled accounting. The weight-space result is on historical data; no live performance claim is made.
