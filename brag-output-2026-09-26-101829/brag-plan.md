# Brag Plan: Portfolio — cayleb-james2008

## What is this app?
A live portfolio site where every claim links its own proof: dated evidence, source URLs, SHA-256 hashes, and honest WIP/UNVERIFIED status badges — public work you can audit.

## The angle
Most portfolios assert. This one cites. The video treats the site's honesty labels — WIP · INCOMPLETE · UNVERIFIED · VERIFIED — like a design system, because they are. Polished, quiet confidence: big editorial type, the three real public projects, one real evidence line with its hash.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"A PORTFOLIO" (big) → "where every claim has a receipt." (settles beneath, quieter).

## Key moments (the middle)
- The three real public projects land as editorial cards: dotz (Rust/axum/Tauri multi-agent dashboard), Sophos (with its upstream credit), Industry AI Suite (ten apps, receipts).
- A real evidence line from the site: "SearchLift · evidence SL-SRC-823863169a · sha256 b4ed4de9…01c88 · 0 issues · 0 side effects" — types out like a citation.
- The status badges parade: VERIFIED / UNVERIFIED / WIP · INCOMPLETE presented as stamps — "the badges are the brand."

## Outro / punchline
"Work you can audit." + cayleb-james2008.github.io
