Principles

How I show up on a team.

Simple habits I bring to shipping software — clear status, runnable work, and reviews that stay useful.

  • Start with a runnable path. Clone, install, run — or open a live URL — before anyone has to ask for a walkthrough.
  • Write the real scope. What’s done, what’s next, and what isn’t claimed yet belong in the README.
  • Let CI speak. Green on the default branch is the bar. Red means fix it, then move on.
  • Prefer boring interfaces. Clear inputs and outputs beat clever abstractions when a teammate has to pick up the work.
  • Keep the UI out of the way. Motion can help; it never blocks reading. prefers-reduced-motion kills anime.js motion and keeps the CRT on poster.
  • Ship for phones too. Primary paths work on a narrow 390px viewport without sideways scrolling. Tap targets ≥44px.
