at a glance

What it reviews

MarketBrief is built for analysts who need sourced evidence turned into a dated macro-risk brief with a clear next step. The live path cites 15 actual annual World Bank U.S. GDP observations and summarizes the latest nominal change.

The app checks the dataset's license page at request time and keeps the source with the brief. It does not fetch stock prices, place orders, or trade.

Where a person takes over

An analyst checks the release timing, adds evidence for the exact question, and reviews each claim before use. The brief is a starting point for that review, not a recommendation.

GDP history is public context, not company research, a securities feed, or evidence of a trading edge. Publication timing and any license override beyond the observed page stay unverified, and the full brief job remains unverified.

what is not verified

  • Public GDP history only; no company, securities, or price data.
  • No order, trade, or investment action capability exists.
  • The full analyst brief stays unverified until licensed research inputs and an analyst decision are supplied.
  • An optional AI summary stays an unwitnessed candidate until someone checks it.
