at a glance

What it reviews

SentinelDesk reads the public CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities list and orders real records by their own due date, date added, and record ID. It returns a 25-record review queue plus a fingerprint of the full response.

The app does not create or infer severity scores or organizational exposure. If a due date is missing, invalid, or needs privacy screening, it reports the source as unavailable and does not invent a deadline.

Where a person takes over

A security defender checks the public record against approved internal sources and decides what, if anything, to do. Containment and account changes are not available in the tool.

The public list is not the organization's own alerts, incidents, or affected assets. The full security response stays unverified without an authorized alert source and an asset inventory.

what is not verified

  • Public vulnerability list only; no company alerts, assets, or incident history were read.
  • No severity score, exposure rating, or affected-asset match is inferred.
  • No containment, account change, or response action is available.
  • The full security response stays unverified until an authorized alert source and asset inventory exist.
