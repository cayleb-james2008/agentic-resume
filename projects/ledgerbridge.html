at a glance

What it reviews

LedgerBridge is built for finance staff who need each cash or ledger row counted exactly once, exceptions explained, and unresolved items sent to a reviewer. The supported live path applies that workflow to public Treasury operating-cash rows only.

The command fetches the public rows, matches them by source date and category, and marks single-sided activity for a person. It never falls back to sample data and it cannot post an entry.

Where a person takes over

A finance reviewer reads the exceptions and decides what to check next. In a real close, that person also needs the company books and the matching bank statement.

The public cash feed is not a company ledger, a bank statement, or a month-end close. The full private-finance job stays unverified until authorized company records are available.

what is not verified

  • Public Treasury rows only; no company books or bank statement were read.
  • No posting, payment, or money movement is possible.
  • The private finance close stays unverified until authorized company records are supplied.
  • An optional AI summary stays an unwitnessed candidate until someone checks it.
