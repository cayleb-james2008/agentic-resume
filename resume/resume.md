### OpenBell - Live Trading House: Slot Method & Weight-Space Models

Python, Rust

[OpenBell case study](https://cayleb-james2008.github.io/agentic-resume/projects/openbell.html)

- All of my trading and finance work under one house: Kalshi prediction markets, a futures desk and a meme-coin desk, run on one architecture — parallel strategy slots splitting the equity evenly, each slot iterating on measured results and getting wiped and replaced when it fails.

- Weight-space programme: instead of training black-box models, I hand-author tiny model weights directly (linear specialists), freeze the prediction in writing before scoring, and judge on strict rules — no money test until the training win rate clears the breakeven for its exit shape. Conventional distillation failed on both test instruments; the hand-authored weights passed, and the best configuration was net-positive on historical data. Live desks run small real capital with reconciled accounting and pre-registered kill conditions.
