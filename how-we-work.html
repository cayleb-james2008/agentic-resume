One company, start to finish

We do more than sell furniture. The same company designs the rooms, prices them on contract, orders everything and installs it with our own crew. Our service team looks after bleachers, backboards and partitions for years after.

Who works on your project

  1. Account executive

    One account executive is assigned to your project from start to finish. They learn what your project and your building need, give advice and recommendations, help you settle on a solution and put the plan in motion.

    At Crosskey Architects, after a burst pipe flooded the studio, our account executive assessed what had to be replaced, specified the new furniture and handled the delivery and installation. See the project

  2. Interior designers

    Our on-staff interior designers have hands-on experience in architecture and design. They work alongside your architect and designer, or directly with you, on layouts, finishes and furniture.

    For the Rockville Public Library addition we did the interior design together with the library staff. See the project

  3. Estimators

    Our estimators price budgets, bids, proposals and final quotes. They know the Connecticut State Contract, MHEC and the other purchasing contracts, so you get contract pricing where you qualify.

    How buying on contract works

  4. Project managers

    Project managers place every order promptly and accurately, and plan the logistics of each installation with you and our installers.

    At Middlebury College’s Virtue Field House they verified the construction dimensions, took the shop drawings through approval, and oversaw and coordinated the installation. See the project

  5. Installers

    Our in-house team of trained furniture installers assembles and sets the furniture we sell, efficiently and on time. Bleachers, seating and gym equipment go in with our manufacturer-certified installers.

    Meet the install crew

  6. Service team

    Telescoping bleachers, overhead basketball backboards and operable partitions are large, heavy equipment, and they can be dangerous if nobody inspects and maintains them. Our service team inspects your equipment, does the maintenance and makes the repairs that keep people safe.

    Safety is the first and last word with this equipment.
