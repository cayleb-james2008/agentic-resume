What it does

The suite groups ten small review workflows across finance, research, support, people operations, security, and discovery. Each path shows its source, result, uncertainty, and next human action. The hosted lab replays ten recorded journeys; five include an independently witnessed, source-cited local model sample.

How to try it

Fastest: open the recorded lab and choose a journey. It is a dated replay, not a live feed. For a fresh local review, follow the public repository's First run guide; it documents python3 -m webapp.server --port 18340 and the local workbench. The project README says the recorded journeys and local review paths do not require a paid API.

Organization-import paths require permitted, de-identified input. The local importer does not authenticate the source or the person providing it.
