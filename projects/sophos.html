Independent engineering project · October 13, 2026

Sophos

A desktop app for coding with AI agents, connected to saved profiles and to AI models on your own computer.

Profile to session

Picking a saved profile starts a real agent session with that profile's instructions. The app makes clear which settings the agent actually follows.

Local model setup

Find or add an AI model running on your own computer and make it available to the agent. The agent service can summarize a session's context. If changing providers fails, the app keeps the previous settings and shows a clear error.
