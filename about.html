Since 1967

Bob Lord founded the company in 1967 to furnish schools. We still do, along with colleges, libraries, offices, town buildings, athletic facilities and healthcare buildings across New England.

Who we are

We work in partnership with your project team to create spaces that help your building, and the people in it, work better. We work closely with everyone involved so that each project, whatever its size, is cost-effective and successful.

Classrooms and their furniture are not what they were 50 years ago. We keep up with how teaching and learning have changed, and we offer furniture that supports it. Our work with schools led us into public libraries, commercial offices and other specialized spaces.

  • Higher education
  • K-12
  • Independent schools
  • Libraries
  • Corporate
  • Municipal
  • Athletic
  • Healthcare

Two divisions, one company

Each division has its own account executives, estimators and project managers. Both work the same way: one account executive stays on your project from start to finish.

Furniture

Classrooms, libraries, offices, cafeterias and common spaces. Our on-staff interior designers support architects, designers and the people who will use the rooms, and our in-house installers set it all up.

Led by Rob Romay, Vice President of Furniture, Fixtures & Equipment

Architectural products

Telescopic bleachers, stadium and auditorium seating, basketball backboards, divider curtains, batting cages and scoreboards. We are the exclusive New England dealer for Hussey Seating, Porter Athletic and All American Scoreboards, and our team is trained by those manufacturers.

Led by Roy McNally, Vice President of Operations & Architectural Products

Our showroom

In 2014 we renovated our headquarters at 220 Chapel Road in Manchester. We designed it and acted as our own general contractor. The 8,000 sq ft showroom and open office are furnished with lines we carry.
