What Asmodeus is

Asmodeus is a strategy research and paper-trading system with human-gated live trading. It researches candidate futures strategies, tests them against costs and overfitting, and replays validated candidates on paper. It was rebuilt from EvoHedge to keep the trading math while replacing the database, queue and desktop stack with one native app and one SQLite file.

Its central bet is that strategies are data, not code. The part that executes them is frozen. The self-improvement side can discover, back-test and promote new strategies, but it can never edit the code that runs them. Every candidate has to survive a set of hard checks before it touches even paper capital.

Verified implementation and safety

Research and paper simulation are implemented. The Rust workspace supports recent-history research, chronological evaluation and durable paper replay, and the 15-part workspace is the active implementation. Paper is the default. Real-money submission is a separate, explicit operator switch, and the repository's checks do not establish whether any deployed copy is armed or uses real capital.

The one-minute and five-minute research runs are paper-only. Any live promotion on longer time frames stays behind operator controls, and the repository makes no profitability claim. A file-based kill switch, a drain switch and automatic circuit breakers sit above the trading path, and the native app runs only while it is open.

what is not verified

  • Installing on Windows and getting live AI answers are still unverified.
  • The repository's checks do not establish whether any deployed copy is armed or uses real capital.
  • No profitability or performance claim is made; research and paper replay do not establish profitable results.
  • The code repository is private; this page is the public record.
  • The app runs only while it is open; there is no keep-alive, scheduled task or reboot recovery.
