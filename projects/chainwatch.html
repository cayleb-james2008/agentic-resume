Industry AI Suite · watch-only EVM review

ChainWatch

Watches crypto wallet activity you supply and flags anything unusual to a person. It cannot move money.

  • Local EVM input
  • Watch-only alerts
  • No wallet access
  • Human escalation

What it does

Takes permitted EVM observations, CSV or JSON. Checks Base or Ethereum addresses. Compares rates to a supplied baseline. Short windows marked uncertain. Breaches go to a human review queue. The importer asks for authorization; the organization still verifies it.

Hosted example and source scope

Lab: two invented observations, to show the math. No chain request. No company address. The local processor works; no external EVM feed or company exposure result is connected.

For your org: the owner confirms the address, sampling history, source rights and escalation authority before any alert counts.

How it was made

Plain rules. No AI used.

External source boundary

No live EVM feed. The Mempool endpoints reviewed earlier serve Bitcoin, not EVM. A real source and address need their own permission and ownership checks.

Human handoff

A treasury operator verifies permitted observations and authorizes the exact address first. No signing, transfer or automated response.
