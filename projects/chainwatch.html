What it reviews

ChainWatch is built for watch-only checks of wallet observations and has no ability to move money. The live command currently returns unverified, because the reviewed public endpoints serve Bitcoin while the tool's own input format expects Ethereum addresses.

Both candidate endpoints answered during review, but the hosted terms page returned only a JavaScript shell, so the terms could not be verified. The app makes no chain request and admits no transactions.

Where a person takes over

The record includes a public nonprofit donation address, published by its owner. That is not a Cayleb/company address and not cryptographic proof of ownership, and company exposure remains unverified.

A person would need to confirm a permitted source, the exact address, the sampling history, and who handles an alert before any observation counts. No signing key, wallet, transfer, or transaction capability exists.
