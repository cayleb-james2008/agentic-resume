at a glance

What it reviews

SearchLift reads one fixed live public page (the owner's portfolio) and reports what its returned title, description, headings, and paragraphs contain. The recorded read is the portfolio page on 2026-09-25, which returned 10/10 approved workflow names.

The result stays within stated limits: 20 headings, 30 paragraphs, and 1,000 characters per item. Text that is omitted or cut short was not assessed, and results never echo the page copy.

Where a person takes over

The portfolio content owner reviews the findings and any source-linked draft. Publishing is a separate decision that this app does not take.

The read is one page at one time. It does not establish unseen content, other routes, rankings, traffic, analytics, or AI completion, and it is not a search-rank guarantee or a measured business outcome.

what is not verified

  • One page and one recorded read; other routes and unseen content were not assessed.
  • No ranking, traffic, analytics, or search-result measurement.
  • No content is edited or published.
  • A source failure reports the source as unavailable or unverified, with no snapshot fallback.
